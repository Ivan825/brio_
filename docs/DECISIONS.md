| D-51 | Thompson sampling gives each agent two forced tries per task type (warm-up) before pure sampling; convergence is tested over 200 seeds per priority with teams whose best agent leads by ≥ 0.15 utility: the best agent is identified in ≥ 90% of runs and is the most-picked over tasks 41–50 in ≥ 85% | Decided (M4) | Without a warm-up, one early failure (1 of 2) starves a strong agent for dozens of tasks; the cost is bounded (2 tries per agent and type) | ε-greedy; UCB; a larger prior |
